# Verity v1.1 benchmark

Generated by `uv run python scripts/bench.py` from the hashed snapshot in `data/snapshot/`
(verify with `verity verify data/snapshot/MANIFEST.json`). No network, no model, same numbers every run.

Data: UK Department for Culture, Media & Sport payments over £25,000, April 2024 - March 2026
(contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0);
USAspending.gov contract transactions, National Science Foundation, FY2025 (public domain);
CORD v2 receipt labels (CC BY 4.0, Park et al. 2019).

## Headline

On **2,931 anomalies injected into real public payments and receipts**, Verity caught **48.8% fully automatically** (a blocking control fired, no human involved) and **69.1% with assistance** (raised for a reviewer).

- Anomaly types the controls are built for: 70.4% automatic, 99.7% assisted (2,031 cases).
- Types no control is built for, included on purpose: 0.0% automatic, 0.2% assisted (900 cases).
- Independent labelled corpus (`fraud_eval`, labels written separately from the rules): caught 7/7 documents carrying fraud a control covers (100%).

## False alarms on untouched real data

| Dataset | Documents | Pass | Review | Blocked | Controls fired |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| uk_dcms | 2,819 | 2,110 | 604 | 105 | P-002×67, P-010×603, P-011×2, P-015×1, P-016×71 |
| usaspending | 709 | 607 | 94 | 8 | P-002×4, P-010×96, P-016×4 |

CORD receipts (genuine, human-labelled): the arithmetic control P-007 fires on 79 of 845 receipts without discounts or extra charges (9.3%), and on 115 of all 972 (127 carry discounts the field schema cannot express).

What the flags on real data are: P-002 pairs are same-supplier, same-amount, same-day payments under different transaction numbers. They are held for a person to confirm, not declared fraud; the public files cannot say which are genuine. P-010 on government data is mostly round grant payments, which is why it is a review-level signal and never blocks. P-016 blocks (41 UK, 4 US) are the same supplier paid the identical amount again within a week under a different transaction number (for example, four payments of £36,695.29 to Historic England between 19 and 22 July 2024). Many will be agreed instalments; that is a question for a person with the contract, which is where Verity sends them.
